## The short version

- **No ads. No trackers. No analytics.** The guide page makes no hidden network requests and loads no third-party scripts.
- **We never sell, rent or trade your information.** Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to "business partners."
- **What you type into your guide stays on your device.** Checkmarks, doctor answers and logged results are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.

The rest of this policy explains each point in plain language.

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the patient guides we build and host (each one a "Guide") and any related pages we run.

If your Guide was provided to you **by your doctor's office**, that office is also responsible for your health information under its own Notice of Privacy Practices. See section 8.

## 2. What information is involved, and where it lives

### 2.1 The content of your Guide

A Guide is built from health information that you, or your doctor's office with your permission, give us. That can include:
- appointments and providers
- medications
- test results
- symptoms
- questions for your care team

This information appears on your Guide page, which is hosted for us by Vercel (see section 5).

**Unlisted links aren't the same as private.** Each Guide is published at a long, unlisted web address and is set so search engines don't index it. Anyone who has the address, though, can open it. You decide whether to add password protection, and we'll turn it on at your request. Treat your Guide link like a key: share it only with people you trust.

### 2.2 What you type into your Guide

When you check off a task, record a doctor's answer, log a new result, or change the text size or theme, that information is saved in your **browser's local storage on your own device**. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it.

This also means:
- Clearing your browser data erases it.
- It doesn't move between your phone and your computer.
- Anyone who uses your unlocked device in the same browser can see it.

### 2.3 Server logs

Like any website host, Vercel automatically records basic technical details when a page is loaded: IP address, browser type, date and time, and the page requested. These logs are used to keep the service running and secure, and are handled under Vercel's own privacy policy. We don't combine them with your health information, and we don't use them to build profiles.

### 2.4 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you. Please don't send detailed health information by regular email; ask us for a secure method instead.

### 2.5 What we don't collect

- No cookies for tracking or advertising.
- No analytics or "session replay" tools.
- No social media pixels.
- No location tracking. The Directions button opens Google Maps only when you tap it, and from then on Google's privacy policy applies.
- No microphone or camera access.

To build or update your Guide, we read the records you provide (such as screenshots of your patient portal or printed visit summaries). We use AI tools to help organize them.

We commit to the following:

- **For Guides provided through a doctor's office,** health information is processed only through services covered by a signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (section 8).
- We only use AI services whose terms prohibit using your content to train their models.
- We use your information only to build, update and support **your** Guide.
- A person reviews every Guide before it's published.

## 4. Zero-knowledge encryption (for accounts and syncing)

We're building optional accounts so your Guide and your notes can sync safely between devices. Accounts will use **zero-knowledge encryption**:

1. You create a password that **never leaves your device**.
2. Your browser turns that password into an encryption key.
3. Your information is encrypted on your device with **AES-256**, a strong standard in widespread use, **before** it's sent anywhere.
4. Our database stores only scrambled data that nobody can read without your password, including us and our hosting providers.

**The trade-off.** Because we can't read your data, **we can't reset your password or recover your data** if you lose it. When you create an account, you'll get a one-time **recovery key**. Print it or store it somewhere safe.

Until accounts launch, no Guide content is stored in a database. It exists only in the published Guide page and in your own browser.

We share information only in these limited cases:

| With | Why | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Vercel** (website hosting) | To publish your Guide page | The Guide page itself and standard server logs |
| **Our AI provider** | To help organize your records into a Guide | The records you provide, under the commitments in section 3 |
| **Our database host**, once accounts launch | To store your encrypted data | Encrypted data only, which it can't read |
| **Your doctor's office** | Only if the office provided your Guide, or you ask us to share | What's needed to keep your Guide accurate |
| **Authorities** | Only when legally required, such as a valid court order | The minimum the law requires; we'll tell you unless the law forbids it |

**We will never:**
- sell, rent or trade your information
- share it with advertisers, data brokers or marketing partners
- use it to target ads to you, anywhere

If our business is ever sold or merged, this policy's promises go with your information. We'll tell you before any change, and you can have your Guide deleted first.

## 6. How long we keep information

- **Guide pages:** until you ask us to delete them, or 12 months after your last request for an update, whichever comes first. We'll email you before deleting for inactivity.
- **Emails with us:** up to 2 years, then deleted.
- **Information in your browser:** stays until you clear it. We have no access to it.
- **Records you sent us to build your Guide:** deleted within 30 days after your Guide is published or updated.

When we delete a Guide, we also remove its earlier published versions.

You can ask us to:
- **see** what information we hold about you
- **correct** anything wrong in your Guide

## 8. When a doctor's office provides your Guide (HIPAA)

If a medical practice hires us to provide Guides to its patients, the practice is a "covered entity" under HIPAA (the federal health privacy law), and we act as its **business associate**. In that case:

- We sign a **Business Associate Agreement** with the practice before receiving any patient information.
- We use your health information only to provide your Guide, as that agreement allows.
- Every company we rely on that could access your information must also sign a business associate agreement.
- The practice's **Notice of Privacy Practices** also applies to you. Ask the practice for a copy.

## 9. How we protect information

- All pages are served only over encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Guide pages load no outside scripts. That shuts the door on whole categories of attacks and leaks.
- Strict browser security settings limit what a page is allowed to do.
- Search engines are told not to index Guide pages.
- Multi-factor authentication on every account we use to run the service.
- Access to Guide content is limited to the people building your Guide.
- Zero-knowledge encryption for account data, once accounts launch (section 4).

No system is perfectly secure. We'll keep improving.

---

## 10. If something goes wrong

If we learn that your information was accessed without permission, we'll notify you as quickly as possible, and no later than 45 days after we confirm what happened. That matches Arizona law (A.R.S. § 18-552). Where federal rules require more, such as HIPAA or the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, we'll follow those too.

The notice will say:
- what happened
- what information was involved
- what we're doing about it
- what you can do
